Redline badge demo

A host page with the Redline floating badge injected at the bottom right. Hover it for issue details (fetched from <mount>/api/traces/<id>; with no backend it keeps retrying quietly and falls back to the summary), click it to open the request, drag it around, press × to collapse it (remembered across reloads), and flip your OS theme to check dark mode.

Try: "issue_count": 0 (green dot, no warning), "status": 500 (red dot), "issue_kinds": ["n_plus_one","slow_query"] (shows "N+1 +1").
